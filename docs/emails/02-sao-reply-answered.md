## What the web page revealed

The web page (retrieved 2026-11-09, registered as `EDUHK-SAO-SEN-WEB-2026`) contains far more detail than the leaflet. Key extract:

### Governing principle

> Guided by the principles of equal opportunities, we are dedicated to providing reasonable accommodations/adjustments to students with Disabilities or Special Educational Needs (SEN).

### Access pathway

> Upon requests and provision of sufficient information (including but not limited to medical reports and records if necessary) by individual students, and upon considering the facts and circumstances of individual cases, the University may provide reasonable and appropriate accommodations/adjustments.

### Lecture/Assignment Arrangements (verbatim list)

- Provision of lecture materials in advance
- Use of assistive aids, devices, or software to support learning
- Peer note-taking arrangements
- Instructional support to facilitate participation
- Preferential seating arrangements
- **Extension of assignment deadlines**
- Alternative formats for assignments
- Flexible group work arrangements

### Special Examination Arrangements (verbatim list)

- Special seating arrangements or a separate examination venue
- Extra time allowance
- Modifications to response formats
- Adaptations to question paper formats
- Use of assistive devices (e.g., screen readers, magnifiers, speech-to-text software, hearing aids)
- Supervised breaks during examinations

## Question-by-question resolution

### Q1: Does Academic Accommodation cover coursework deadlines?

**RESOLVED: Yes.**

"Extension of assignment deadlines" is explicitly listed under Lecture/Assignment Arrangements on the SAO support service web page.

This is the single most important finding for the project. The post-deadline extension state previously said the route through the lecturer is closed (GAR 9.6.1 requires advance permission; 9.6.3 → F) and the SEN service exists but its scope is unknown. Now we know the SEN service explicitly covers assignment deadline extensions.

### Q2: Are TPG/RPG students eligible?

**RESOLVED: The page does not restrict by programme.**

The page addresses "students with Disabilities or SEN" at the University without stating programme-level restrictions. This is consistent with the leaflet, which also does not restrict. The scope_basis remains `needs_confirmation` because the page does not explicitly say "all programmes" — it just does not restrict.

### Q3: Can accommodation be arranged after a deadline has passed?

**STILL OPEN.**

The page lists "Extension of assignment deadlines" as a possible accommodation but does not state whether requests can be made retrospectively. SAO did not answer this question in the reply.

If a follow-up is needed, the question should be asked in isolation: "Can a request for an assignment deadline extension be considered after the original deadline has passed, or must it be requested in advance?"
